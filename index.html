4B.2 Differentiate RTA vs GI Cause
➕ If UAG is POSITIVE (Always Distal!):
  • ↓/Nl K⁺ + Urine pH > 5.5: Distal RTA (Type 1) (Sjögren, SLE, stones, AmphoB)
  • ↑ Serum K⁺ (Hyperkalemia): Distal RTA (Type 4) (Hypoaldo, DM, ACEi/ARB, NSAIDs)
➖ If UAG is NEGATIVE (Intact Distal NH₄⁺):
  • GI Bicarb Loss: Diarrhea or Laxative abuse (Urine K⁺ < 20)
  • Urine pH < 5.3 + Fanconi/MM/Meds: Proximal RTA (Type 2) (Acetazolamide, Topiramate, Tenofovir)
